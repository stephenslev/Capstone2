Northstar Analytics Performance Management Policy

Document owner: People Operations
Version: 2.0
Effective date: April 1, 2025
Applies to: All employees and people managers

Purpose

Performance management aligns individual contribution with customer outcomes, team commitments, company priorities, and professional development. It is intended to provide clarity and timely feedback rather than surprise employees during formal review periods.

Goal setting

Employees and managers establish role-relevant goals at the beginning of the performance cycle. Goals should describe the expected outcome, measure of progress, dependencies, and how quality or customer impact will be assessed. Revenue goals should be balanced with retention, customer experience, quality, and operational health where relevant.

Check-ins

Managers conduct regular check-ins to review progress, priorities, workload, feedback, and development. Goals may be revised when business priorities or dependencies change. Material changes should be documented so that employees are evaluated against realistic expectations.

Calibration

People Operations facilitates calibration to improve consistency across teams. Calibration should consider results, behaviors, complexity, collaboration, customer impact, and documented context. A single metric should not determine an overall performance assessment.

Development and support

Employees who need additional support should receive clear expectations, practical coaching, resources, and a reasonable review period. Managers should address concerns promptly and document significant feedback. Employees may involve People Operations when expectations are unclear or when they believe the process is inconsistent.

Customer and team outcomes

Performance discussions should include relevant customer outcomes, quality indicators, collaboration, and risk management. Employees should not be rewarded for short-term results that create avoidable customer harm, control failures, or sustained workload problems.
